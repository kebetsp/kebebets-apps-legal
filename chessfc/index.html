Privacy Policy — Chess FC

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Chess FC ("the app") is a chess-and-football board game for iPhone and iPad, published by Pavel Kebets ("I", "me").

Short version

The app does not collect, send, share or sell any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores

  • Your current game and your match settings (for example difficulty and time control), so you can continue after closing the app.

This data is kept only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not connect to the internet or to any server. The computer opponent runs entirely on your device.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Third parties

Apple distributes the app and may process information about your download and device under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). I do not receive personal information from Apple beyond anonymous aggregate statistics available in App Store Connect.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this page with an updated date. Any change that involves collecting data would come with an app update and an updated App Store privacy label.
