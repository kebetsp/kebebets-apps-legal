Balanced City - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Balanced City ("the game") is a puzzle-style city building game published by Pavel Kebets. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Short version

The game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

What stays on your device

The game saves your current city and your settings (such as vibration and reduced motion) in the app's private storage on your phone, so you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No location data, contacts, photos or microphone/camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sent to third parties.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle personal information (for example online leaderboards or purchases), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the date above will change.
